Questions

The things parents ask first.

You're still at school yourself. Why you?

Because we sat these exact papers recently, got grade 9s in them, and still remember where the marks actually go — and we're both being examined at a higher level than we teach. I'm not a qualified teacher and I don't pretend to be one. What I offer is current, specific subject knowledge and exam technique, one to one. The free first lesson exists so you can judge that before any money changes hands.

Who would actually be teaching my child?

One of two people, depending on the subject. Archit takes Maths, Further Maths and Physics; Raniya takes Chemistry, Maths, Further Maths and the ESAT. Either of us can help with a personal statement. You'll know who before you book, and the same person teaches every lesson — nobody gets passed around.

What are your actual grades?

Archit: grade 9 in Maths, Further Maths and Physics at GCSE — 97%, 95% and 96%. Predicted A* in Maths (Edexcel), A* in Physics (AQA), A* in Economics and an A in Further Maths (OCR MEI), applying to Cambridge and LSE for Economics.

Raniya: ten grade 9s at GCSE, three A*s and an A at A-level, now reading Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology at Cambridge. Happy to show certificates on request.

What happens in a lesson?

A video call with a shared digital whiteboard, so we're both writing on the same page. We start with last week's questions, work through the target topic, then finish on timed exam questions. Annotated notes go out afterwards so there's a record to revise from.

Which exam boards do you cover?

AQA, Edexcel and OCR for Maths and Physics. For Further Maths, the AQA Level 2 Certificate and OCR Additional Maths. Tell me the board when you enquire and I'll work from that specification.

Do you set homework?

Yes — usually one timed past-paper section. It comes back marked against the official mark scheme with every lost mark explained, because knowing why a mark went is the part that changes the grade.

Do you tutor A-level?

Not as a rule. Get in touch about AS Maths or Further Maths and we'll tell you honestly whether we can help. The personal statement and ESAT coaching is sixth-form work, though — that's for applicants, not GCSE students.
