Where are your marks actually going?

Most students who get a 6 instead of an 8 don't have a maths problem. They have a writing-it-down problem. Six questions, twenty marks — this tells you which one you have.

6questions 20marks ~15minutes
0 of 6 marked

Do these on paper first. Work each one out properly, the way you would in an exam — then type your final answer in.

After you check an answer you'll see the mark scheme split into method marks and accuracy marks. Tick only the lines you actually wrote down. Being honest here is the entire point; nobody sees this but you.
